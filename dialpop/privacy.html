Dial Pop Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-11-07 · Lagoonworks

This is the standalone privacy policy for the mobile game "Dial Pop" by Lagoonworks.

1. Summary

Dial Pop has no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number. To keep the game free we show ads (Google AdMob), to improve the game we collect anonymous play logs, and for leaderboards we send scores to Google Play Games or Apple Game Center.

2. Information we collect

What Why Where How long
A random install ID (created on first launch, not linked to your name or advertising ID) To group play logs by device Google Firebase Until you delete the app or ask us to delete it, at most 2 years
Anonymous play logs (score, run length, security level, revive and ad counts, app version, device model, OS version) To tune difficulty and ad frequency, and fix bugs Google Firebase At most 2 years
Remote config requests To adjust game balance without an app update Google Firebase Not stored

Play logs are explained on the title screen and can be turned off at the bottom of the How to Play screen. The game works the same with them off.

3. Information kept only on your device

Best scores, weekly bests, settings (sound, vibration, ring count) and games saved with "Save & exit" stay on your device. They are not sent to us and are removed when you delete the app.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

Dial Pop shows rewarded ads (to revive) and interstitial ads (every few games). Google may collect the following directly:

  • Advertising ID (Android advertising ID; iOS IDFA only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and OS information, approximate location derived from IP, ad views and clicks

Google processes this under its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears on first launch. You can refuse personalized ads and still play.
  • On iPhone, the "Allow tracking" prompt appears. If you decline, IDFA is not used.
  • On Android, you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.

5. Leaderboards and achievements (Google Play Games / Apple Game Center)

If you use leaderboards or achievements, your scores and achievement progress are sent to Google Play Games (Android) or Apple Game Center (iPhone). Leaderboards show the nickname and profile you set in that service, which follows its own policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple Privacy Policy

You can play without signing in.

6. Service providers

We do not sell personal information. These companies process data for the purposes above:

Company Role
Google LLC (Firebase) Anonymous play logs, remote config
Google LLC (AdMob) Ads, ad consent form
Google LLC (Play Games) Android leaderboards and achievements
Apple Inc. (Game Center) iPhone leaderboards and achievements

Their servers may be outside your country (mostly in the United States).

7. Deleting your data

  • On-device data: delete the app.
  • Anonymous play logs: send the install ID shown in the How to Play screen (? button) to support@lagoonworks.app and we will delete it within 30 days. Without the ID we cannot tell which logs are yours.
  • Ad data: manage it in your Google Ad Settings.
  • Leaderboard data: manage it in Google Play Games or Game Center settings.

8. Children

Dial Pop is not directed at children under 13. We do not ask for age and do not knowingly collect children's information. If you believe a child's information was sent to us, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

All transfers use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Play logs contain no names or contact details. Access to stored logs is limited to the developer.

10. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or to stop its processing, at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA) you may have additional rights. Contact us below.

11. Contact

Lagoonworks · support@lagoonworks.app

12. Changes

We post changes on this page at least 7 days in advance, and 30 days in advance (with an in-app notice) if we start collecting more information.
