OVERMIND

OVERMIND

A little island at the top of your screen

See the features

It rests quietly as a little island at the top of your screen and opens when you move the mouse over it. News from the other family apps gathers there as pills, and your schedule, to-dos, music and AI live in one place. A character called Ovi lives on the island.

Download · coming soonWindows 10·11 · in testingmacOS · coming soon

Features

The island at a glance

See the weather, time, Wi-Fi and battery right on the little island. While it rests, it uses almost no power.

Notification pills

News from Parser, Optic and Glen shows up as pills. Click to open or reply right away.

Schedule, D-day and to-dos

Count down to upcoming events and D-days, and keep to-dos and notes on the island.

Focus timer

Stay focused for a set time, and get told when to take a break.

Music

Pause and skip the song that is playing, right from the island.

Clipboard history

Find text you copied earlier and paste it again.

Recording and dictation

Record and turn speech into text.

Notch AI

Ask questions on the island with your own AI key. Keys stay only in your OS secure storage.

One place for AI keys

Add an AI key to the notch once, and Parser and Optic use it too.

Privacy

AI keys stay only in your OS secure storage (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain). Only what you choose is sent to AI. See the Privacy Policy for details.
