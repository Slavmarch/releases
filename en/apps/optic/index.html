Optic

Optic

Turn a screen capture into text

See the features

Press the shortcut and drag a box on your screen. AI reads the text inside and puts it straight into your clipboard, ready to paste.

Download · coming soonWindows 10·11 · in testingmacOS · coming soon

Features

One shortcut

Ctrl+Alt+Z freezes the screen so you can pick what to read. You can change the shortcut.

True to the source

Keeps Korean, English abbreviations, Hanja and tables exactly as they are.

Many captures in a row

Capture several areas of the same size, then copy them all at once or save them as txt.

Read photos

Drop in several photos and turn them into text one by one.

Choose your AI

Pick Gemini, Claude or GPT, and switch to another model when one is busy.

Results on the island

Results appear on the OVERMIND island, and you can copy them again from there.

Privacy

AI keys stay only in your OS secure storage (Windows Credential Manager, macOS Keychain). Only what you choose is sent to AI. See the Privacy Policy for details.
